About

Thirty years underneath the Pacific Northwest's growth.

Bob Carpenter has spent his career on the civil side of development — the grading, drainage, utilities, and street work that sits underneath finished subdivisions, port terminals, and school campuses.

After moving to the Pacific Northwest and starting a family in 1993, he spent twelve years in the private engineering and surveying community before founding Carpenter Engineering, Inc. in 2005. The firm was built around direct, client-facing work: one licensed engineer accountable for a project from feasibility and permitting through construction drawings and field oversight.

That range shows in the work collected here. It spans a 57-lot residential subdivision in Clark County, waterfront parking and trail improvements for the Port of Kalama, pavement and pedestrian-safety programs for Longview's schools and streets, and spill-prevention plans for public utility districts across Washington and Oregon.

Capabilities

What the practice covered.

Six areas of civil work, drawn from the projects in this portfolio rather than from a service brochure.

01

Land Development

Subdivision layout, lot grading, frontage widening, and the street and utility design that turns raw acreage into buildable lots.

02

Stormwater Design

Collection, conveyance, treatment, and detention — bio-retention, sand filters, underground infiltration galleries, and duplex pump stations.

03

Site & Civil Design

Grading and drainage, drive lanes, parking, retaining walls, and building-pad design for commercial and light industrial sites.

04

Utility Infrastructure

Sewer and water main extensions, force mains, substation and pole-yard grading, fuel-island containment, and oil/water separation.

05

Permitting & Planning

Jurisdictional coordination, feasibility review, and the plan sets and calculations needed to carry a project through agency approval.

06

Construction Oversight

Construction documents, contractor coordination, progress review, and the field decisions that keep an approved design buildable.
